# Questionnaires and Measures

## NASA-TLX (Cognitive Load)
Use the NASA Task Load Index to measure perceived mental demand, physical demand, temporal demand, effort, frustration, and performance.

### Sample items
- How mentally demanding was the task?
- How hurried or rushed was the pace of the task?
- How successful were you in accomplishing what you were asked to do?
- How hard did you have to work to accomplish your level of performance?
- How insecure, discouraged, irritated, stressed, and annoyed were you?

### Scoring
- Each dimension is rated on a 7-point or 21-point scale depending on instrument version
- Weighted or unweighted scores can be used
- Lower scores indicate lower workload

## SUS (System Usability Scale)
Use the SUS to evaluate usability of the AR experience and learning interface.

### Example statements
1. I think that I would like to use this system frequently.
2. I found the system unnecessarily complex.
3. I thought the system was easy to use.
4. I would need the support of a technical person to be able to use this system.
5. I found the various functions in this system were well integrated.
6. I thought there was too much inconsistency in this system.
7. I would imagine that most people would learn to use this system very quickly.
8. I found the system very cumbersome to use.
9. I felt very confident using the system.
10. I needed to learn a lot of things before I could get going with this system.

### Scoring
- Responses are on a 5-point Likert scale
- Raw scores are converted to a 0-100 SUS composite index
- A score above ~68 is generally considered above average usability

## Additional Measures
- Pre/post knowledge test
- Spatial reasoning assessment
- Health literacy retention task
- Qualitative interview prompts

### Example qualitative prompts
- What part of the interface was most helpful for learning?
- What felt confusing or difficult?
- Which learning cue most supported your understanding?
- How did the AR timeline affect recall of health events or concepts?

## Administration Timing
- Baseline demographics: before the intervention
- NASA-TLX: immediately after the learning task
- SUS: immediately after the intervention
- Retention and knowledge tests: immediately post-test and follow-up assessment
