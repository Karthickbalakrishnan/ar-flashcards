# Results and Analysis

## Preliminary Analysis Framework
This document outlines a simple preliminary analysis structure for evaluating the pilot or early-stage results of the study.

## Primary Outcomes
1. Spatial reasoning gain score
2. Health literacy retention score
3. Cognitive load (NASA-TLX)
4. Usability (SUS)

## Suggested Statistical Approach
- Descriptive statistics for all conditions
- Mean and standard deviation for pre-test, post-test, and follow-up scores
- Paired comparisons within groups
- Between-group comparisons using t-tests or ANOVA if sample size supports it
- Effect size reporting (Cohen's d or eta squared)

## Example Reporting Structure
### Participant Summary
- N = sample size
- Age and education distribution
- Prior experience with AR or 3D visualization

### Learning Outcomes
- Intervention groups show greater improvement in spatial reasoning than control groups
- AR timeline conditions show stronger recall of health concepts across delayed follow-up
- Gains may be moderated by cognitive load and usability perceptions

### Cognitive Load and Usability
- Lower NASA-TLX values indicate reduced cognitive burden
- Higher SUS scores indicate better perceived usability
- Higher usability may correlate with stronger learning performance

## Example Preliminary Findings (Template)
- 3D visualization outperformed 2D controls on spatial reasoning tasks by X%
- AR timeline participants demonstrated improved retention of health chronology and key instructional concepts
- Participants in AR conditions reported moderate cognitive load but high usability scores
- Qualitative feedback suggested that spatial manipulation and temporal cues improved understanding and recall

## Interpretation
Early findings should be treated as preliminary evidence. The study is intended to establish feasibility, identify design issues, and estimate effect sizes for future larger-scale evaluation.

## Recommended Next Steps
- Expand sample size for stronger statistical power
- Refine instructions and interface design based on usability findings
- Validate assessment instruments for reliability
- Conduct a larger randomized study with delayed follow-up and subgroup analysis
