# Research Hypothesis

## H1
3D molecular visualization improves spatial reasoning by X% compared with traditional 2D molecular diagrams, as measured by post-test performance on spatial tasks and molecule manipulation accuracy.

## H2
AR + timeline improves health literacy retention by increasing the ability to remember and correctly sequence health events, risk factors, and preventive actions over time.

## Study Context
This research examines the combined effect of immersive 3D visualization and temporal storytelling for learning in STEM and health literacy contexts. The project is designed to test whether visualizing molecular structure in augmented reality and presenting health information along a timeline improves understanding, retention, and user confidence.

## Measurement methodology
A mixed-methods evaluation will use a pre-test, intervention, post-test design with an active control group.

### Design
- Pre-test: baseline assessment of spatial reasoning and health literacy knowledge
- Intervention: exposure to either 3D molecular visualization and/or AR timeline-based learning materials
- Post-test: immediate assessment after the intervention
- Control group: standard instructional materials without AR/3D support
- Follow-up retention assessment: delayed test to quantify learning persistence

### Variables
- Independent variables:
  - visualization format (2D vs 3D)
  - modality (desktop/flat interface vs AR)
  - timeline support (static chronology vs AR timeline)
- Dependent variables:
  - spatial reasoning accuracy
  - health literacy retention score
  - perceived cognitive load
  - usability and engagement

### Analysis plan
- Compare mean changes from pre-test to post-test between intervention and control groups
- Compute retention effects at follow-up using repeated-measures analysis
- Report effect sizes and confidence intervals for all major outcomes
- Use qualitative feedback to explain performance patterns and usability barriers

## Expected Contribution
This work will provide preliminary evidence that immersive, spatial, and temporally structured learning environments improve comprehension and memory retention relative to conventional instructional methods.
